Hi, I'm Bonya.

Hi! I’m an artist and media designer who loves storytelling, portraiture, and design. I work with both hands-on art (like painting and ink drawing) and digital media. My art focuses on human emotion, cultural heritage, and visual mood. I love exploring how color, lines, and lighting change the feeling of a piece. Outside of art, I enjoy listening to music and studying visual media.

My style mixes soft pastel pinks with loud primary colours, hand-made textures with crisp pixels. I want my work to make people smile before they know why.
